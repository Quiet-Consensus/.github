# Quiet Consensus

**Engineering systems that earn trust.**

Quiet Consensus is an independent UK-based technical consultancy working across distributed systems, blockchain protocols and emerging infrastructure.

Our work combines hands-on technical investigation, architecture, prototyping and engineering leadership to help organisations evaluate complex systems and move from research to production.

### Areas of practice

- **Distributed Systems & Infrastructure** — architecture, reliability, performance and operational resilience.
- **Blockchain & Protocol Engineering** — Ethereum infrastructure, consensus, validator operations and protocol integration.
- **AI Infrastructure & Applied Research** — technical experimentation, agentic workflows and infrastructure evaluation.
- **Technical Leadership & Advisory** — architecture reviews, technical due diligence and embedded engineering leadership.

### Research and open source

This organisation hosts selected research, technical prototypes, engineering tools and open-source projects.

Experimental work is labelled accordingly and should not be assumed to be production-ready.
